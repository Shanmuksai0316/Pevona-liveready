# How to Upload Blogs - Simple Guide

This guide will walk you through uploading a new blog post to your website step by step.

## 📋 What You'll Need

Before you start, make sure you have:
- ✅ Your Strapi admin login credentials
- ✅ Your blog post content ready (title, text, images)
- ✅ A featured image for your blog (optional but recommended)

---

## 🚀 Step-by-Step Instructions

### Step 1: Log into Strapi Admin

1. Open your web browser
2. Go to your Strapi admin URL:
   - **Production**: `http://api.pevonaltd.co.uk/admin` or `http://31.97.117.9:1337/admin`
3. Enter your **username** and **password**
4. Click **"Sign in"**

---

### Step 2: Navigate to Blog Section

1. In the left sidebar menu, look for **"Content Manager"**
2. Click on **"Content Manager"**
3. You'll see a list of content types - find and click on **"Blog"**

---

### Step 3: Create a New Blog Post

1. Click the **"+ Create new entry"** button (usually at the top right)
2. A form will open with all the fields you need to fill

---

### Step 4: Fill in the Blog Details

Fill in each field as follows:

#### **Title** (Required) ⭐
- Enter your blog post title
- Example: "10 Tips for First-Time Home Buyers"
- This will appear at the top of your blog post

#### **Slug** (Auto-Generated)
- This is automatically created from your title
- You don't need to change this unless you want a custom URL
- Example: If title is "10 Tips for First-Time Home Buyers", slug becomes "10-tips-for-first-time-home-buyers"

#### **Author** (Optional)
- Enter the author's name
- Example: "John Smith" or "Pevona Team"

#### **Featured Image** (Recommended)
1. Click on the **"Add media"** or **"Click to add an asset"** button
2. Either:
   - **Upload a new image**: Click "Upload files" and select an image from your computer
   - **Use existing image**: Click on an image that's already uploaded
3. Choose an image that represents your blog post
4. **Recommended size**: 1200x600 pixels or similar ratio
5. Click **"Finish"** to select the image

#### **Excerpt** (Optional but Recommended)
- Write a short summary of your blog post (2-3 sentences)
- This appears on the blog listing page
- Example: "Discover essential tips and strategies for first-time home buyers to make confident property investment decisions."

#### **Content** (Required) ⭐
- This is where you write your full blog post
- Use the rich text editor to format your content:
  - **Bold text**: Select text and click **B** button
  - **Italic text**: Select text and click **I** button
  - **Headings**: Use the dropdown to select H2, H3, etc.
  - **Lists**: Click the bullet or numbered list buttons
  - **Links**: Select text, click link icon, enter URL
  - **Images**: Click image icon to add images within the content

**Tips for Content:**
- Break up long paragraphs for better readability
- Use headings (H2, H3) to organize sections
- Add images throughout to make it more engaging
- Use bullet points or numbered lists when appropriate

#### **Category** (Optional)
- Enter a category name
- Examples: "Buying Guide", "Market Insights", "Property Tips", "Investment Advice"
- This appears as a badge on your blog post

#### **Tags** (Optional)
- Click **"Add an entry"** to add tags
- Enter relevant keywords separated by commas
- Examples: "Home Buying", "Real Estate", "Investment", "Property Guide"
- Tags help with search and organization

#### **SEO** (Optional but Recommended)
Click to expand the SEO section:
- **Meta Title**: Short title for search engines (50-60 characters)
- **Meta Description**: Brief description for search results (150-160 characters)
- **Keywords**: Relevant keywords separated by commas

---

### Step 5: Save Your Blog Post

You have two options:

#### **Option A: Save as Draft** (Recommended First)
1. Click **"Save"** button (top right)
2. Your blog is saved but **not published** yet
3. You can come back later to edit and publish

#### **Option B: Publish Immediately**
1. Click the **"Publish"** button (top right)
2. Your blog will be **live on the website immediately**

**💡 Tip**: It's better to save as draft first, review everything, then publish when ready!

---

### Step 6: Review Your Blog Post

1. After publishing, visit your website
2. Go to the **"Blogs"** page
3. Check that:
   - ✅ Your blog appears in the list
   - ✅ The title is correct
   - ✅ The image displays properly
   - ✅ The content looks good
   - ✅ Links work correctly

---

## ✏️ Editing an Existing Blog

1. Go to **Content Manager** → **Blog**
2. Find the blog post you want to edit
3. Click on it to open
4. Make your changes
5. Click **"Save"** or **"Publish"** to update

---

## 🗑️ Deleting a Blog Post

1. Go to **Content Manager** → **Blog**
2. Find the blog post you want to delete
3. Click on it to open
4. Click the **"Delete"** button (usually at the top)
5. Confirm the deletion

**⚠️ Warning**: Deleted blogs cannot be recovered easily. Consider unpublishing instead if you might need it later.

## 🎨 Formatting Tips

### Making Your Blog Look Professional

1. **Use Headings**: Break up your content with H2 and H3 headings
   ```
   Main Section (H2)
   ├── Subsection (H3)
   └── Another Subsection (H3)
   ```

2. **Add Images**: Add relevant images throughout your content
   - Click the image icon in the editor
   - Upload or select an image
   - Add alt text for accessibility

3. **Use Lists**: Make information easy to scan
   - Bullet points for general lists
   - Numbered lists for step-by-step instructions

4. **Bold Important Points**: Highlight key information

5. **Add Links**: Link to relevant pages or external resources

---

## 🖼️ Image Guidelines

### Featured Image
- **Recommended size**: 1200x600 pixels (2:1 ratio)
- **File format**: JPG or PNG
- **File size**: Under 500KB for faster loading
- **What to include**: An image that represents your blog topic

### Content Images
- **Recommended size**: 800-1200 pixels wide
- **File format**: JPG or PNG
- **File size**: Under 300KB each
- **Alt text**: Always add descriptive alt text for accessibility

## ❓ Common Questions

### Q: Can I edit a published blog?
**A**: Yes! Just open it, make changes, and click "Publish" again to update.

### Q: What if I make a mistake?
**A**: You can always edit and republish. If you need to hide it temporarily, you can unpublish it.

### Q: How do I add images within the content?
**A**: Click the image icon in the rich text editor, then upload or select an image.

### Q: What's the difference between "Save" and "Publish"?
**A**: 
- **Save**: Saves as draft (not visible on website)
- **Publish**: Makes it live on the website

### Q: Can I schedule a blog to publish later?
**A**: Currently, you need to publish manually. Save as draft and publish when ready.

### Q: How long should my blog post be?
**A**: Aim for at least 500-800 words for good SEO, but quality is more important than length.

## 🆘 Need Help?

If you encounter any issues:

1. **Can't log in**: Contact your administrator for password reset
2. **Images not uploading**: Check file size (should be under 5MB) and format (JPG/PNG)
3. **Content not saving**: Make sure all required fields (Title, Content) are filled
4. **Blog not appearing**: Make sure you clicked "Publish" not just "Save"
